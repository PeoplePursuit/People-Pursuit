ASSESS FOR THE ROLE, NOT JUST THE RESUME

More context. More consistent conversations.

Use assessment tools to add structure to the hiring process—not to replace recruiter judgment.

JD-ALIGNED

Questions shaped around the role

Use a supplied job description to suggest skill areas and draft role-specific questions. Recruiters can edit, reframe, and approve every question before launch.

JD skillStakeholder communicationDraft prompt“Tell us how you aligned different teams around a project decision.”

STRUCTURED INTERVIEWS

AI-assisted interview experience

Offer consistent, role-relevant prompts in a guided interview flow. Interview summaries and competency signals can help a recruiter prepare follow-up questions.

Example interview prompt“Describe a time you used evidence to change your approach.”Illustrative prompt

PROFILE REVIEW

AI-assisted profile screening

Compare candidate-provided experience with job-related criteria, surface relevant evidence, and flag gaps for recruiter review—not an automatic pass or rejection.
