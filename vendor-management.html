END-TO-END VENDOR MANAGEMENT WORKFLOW

Five phases. Clear ownership at each step.

Move supplier relationships from registration to renewal with review points, traceable approvals, and operational handoffs.

Example supplier lifecycle
01

START WITH THE SUPPLIER

Registration & onboarding

Vendor self-service

Procurement or a vendor sends an invitation to a secure external workspace.

  • Collect legal entity, tax/VAT, banking, insurance, and category-specific documents.
  • Support localized fields for applicable certificates, such as MSME or diversity credentials.
  • Validate required formats and missing fields before the profile is submitted.
02

VERIFY BEFORE ENGAGING

Risk & compliance verification

Risk-based checks

Route the right level of diligence based on supplier category, spend, and data exposure.

  • Coordinate sanctions, debarment, and watchlist screening through configured sources.
  • Assign low, medium, or high risk tiers with matching financial, security, or safety reviews.
  • Verify bank ownership through approved verification providers and controlled review steps.
03

REVIEW & ESTABLISH THE RECORD

Internal approvals & ERP sync

Controlled handoff

Send the supplier through the configured internal approval path before activation.

  • Route to Legal, Finance, InfoSec, or business owners based on category, risk, and thresholds.
  • Check for duplicate records and resolve potential matches before creating a supplier profile.
  • After approval, prepare a verified master record for a configured ERP integration.
04

WORK, DELIVER, RECONCILE

Execution, POs & performance

Procure to pay

Keep commercial documents and delivery milestones connected to day-to-day supplier work.

  • Coordinate purchase orders, acknowledgements, shipment notices, and electronic invoices.
  • Link invoices to approved POs, timesheets, goods receipts, or service milestones.
  • Track delivery, invoice accuracy, and service-level indicators in reviewable scorecards.
05

MAINTAIN OR CLOSE RESPONSIBLY

Offboarding & renewal management

Lifecycle maintenance

Keep obligations visible before renewal or relationship closure is due.

  • Notify owners ahead of insurance, contract, license, and tax-certificate expirations.
  • Review performance, outstanding actions, and risk status before renewal decisions.
  • When a relationship ends, coordinate access removal, liability clearance, and audit retention.

TWO PORTALS, ONE CONTROLLED WORKFLOW

The right workspace for every relationship.

Role-based views can give internal teams broad oversight while keeping external partner access scoped to their own work.

INTERNAL WORKSPACE

Company admin portal

Procurement · HR · Finance · Hiring teams
  • Vendor directory, onboarding queues, and compliance alerts.
  • Category and rate-card controls with configurable approval routing.
  • Candidate pipelines, placements, purchase orders, and invoice review.
  • Spend, vendor performance, pending tasks, and audit reporting.
Access shaped by internal role and approval responsibility.

EXTERNAL WORKSPACE

Vendor portal

Staffing partners · Suppliers · Agency teams
  • Maintain the vendor’s own profile, certificates, and renewal dates.
  • Submit candidates only against assigned open requisitions.
  • View the vendor’s own purchase orders and placement status.
  • Submit invoices and track review, payment, and scorecard updates.
Partner access restricted to its own permitted records.

INTELLIGENCE & COLLABORATION

Less chasing. More visibility into what needs attention.

Modern portal concepts can help teams surface exceptions sooner while keeping sensitive changes and decisions under human control.

DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

AI-assisted document review

Extract proposed fields, terms, and expiry dates from uploaded certificates, contracts, or invoices for human verification. Compare invoice lines to PO and receipt data to flag possible mismatches for review.

Suggestions require verification against the source document.

CONTINUOUS RISK

Risk signals & review queues

Connect configured risk sources and internal assessments to bring material changes to a reviewer’s attention. Escalate a flagged supplier before further purchasing steps are taken.

Source coverage and decisions depend on configured policy and providers.

PAYMENT CHANGE CONTROL

Verified bank detail updates

Route sensitive banking changes through authenticated vendor access, independent verification, and designated internal approvals. Keep the change history available for audit.

No bank change should rely on email instructions alone.

PROACTIVE OPERATIONS

Renewal reminders & follow-ups

Schedule configurable reminders for expiring documents and contracts. Draft follow-ups for missing items, delayed shipments, or unresolved actions for an owner to review.

Automated messages and escalations follow workspace rules.

SPEND & PERFORMANCE

Scorecards and pricing insight

Review delivery, invoice accuracy, SLA measures, placement yield, and spend trends. Optional market benchmarks should use appropriate aggregated data and context.

Illustrative metrics are not live vendor performance data.

SHARED WORKSPACE

Supplier collaboration hub

Keep invoice disputes, delivery exceptions, document requests, and resolution notes in a shared, time-stamped conversation visible to the right participants.

Partner views remain scoped to authorized records.

DASHBOARDS & REPORTING

Useful views for admins, partners, and finance.

Put operational queues and relationship health into context with role-appropriate dashboards and vendor-level reports.

Admin: spend, approvals, risk alerts, and vendor performance. Vendor: own submissions, POs, invoice status, and expiring documents. Reports: placement yield, SLA adherence, and financial ledger.
